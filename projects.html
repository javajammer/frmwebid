BagiPDF PDF Editor, Splitter, Converter

A small PDF toolkit for people who would rather keep their documents on their own machine. BagiPDF lets you split, protect, lock, and manage PDF files locally — without sending documents to a cloud service. Built for offline workflows, with zero telemetry. Your documents stay on your device.

Novenaku Catholic Prayer and Local Church Companion

A small Android application built around daily prayer, traditional prayers, novenas, the Rosary, and finding local churches. It includes more than 100 traditional prayers, 50+ novenas, daily Rosary guidance, and information for hundreds of parishes across Jabodetabek. Built as a personal project and shared openly for anyone who finds it useful.

Why I Build

I came to software the long way — through burnout, through rushing, through the belief that faster meant better.

|

Hover to hear me say it

I've spent years building and operating software around security, infrastructure, internal systems, and small tools.

That experience made me appreciate something simple: software doesn't always need to become a company. Sometimes a small tool is enough. Sometimes the best outcome is learning how something works. Sometimes making something useful for ten people is more satisfying than trying to make something impressive for ten thousand.

So I build small things, document what I can, release what feels ready, and let the projects find their own size.

Have something you're trying to figure out?

This isn't a software shop. But if you're working on a small technical problem, an interesting idea, or something that doesn't quite fit into an existing tool, you're welcome to write. Maybe I've built something that helps. Maybe I can point you toward something that already exists. Maybe we'll discover that the problem is more interesting than the solution. Either way, I'm happy to have the conversation.

  • I read every message myself
  • No sales call - No newsletter - No follow-up sequence
  • No promise that I'll build your idea
  • If I can't help, I'll say so — and suggest who might

You'll hear back personally when I can.

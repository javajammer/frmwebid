Who is Muhammad Fahrizal Rahman?

Muhammad Fahrizal Rahman, also known as Rizal, is VP of Infrastructure & Security at PT Infokes Indonesia in Jakarta. He leads a 26-person organization and an infrastructure budget above USD 500k a year, covering technical support, systems administration, DevOps, security, and database administration for a healthcare platform used by about 70% of Indonesia's puskesmas.

What has he delivered?

He led the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS through certification in 2025 and the first surveillance audit in 2026, both with no minor findings. He migrated a 300-server environment from on-premises and Google Cloud to AWS in one month without unexpected downtime, moved Intel servers to ARM by December 2025, cut server cost by 25%, and reduced downtime by about 60% with Lambda-based auto-healing. Security operations include Wazuh SIEM, SentinelOne EDR, Cloudflare WAF, a SOC, custom SOAR, and a PICA-based incident process for P0 to P3 incidents.

Where are professional security engagements handled?

Professional security engagements outside his role at PT Infokes Indonesia are handled by Practical Security. This site remains a personal showcase, not a booking page.
